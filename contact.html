THE AUTHORVEYRA PRINCIPLE

Making the conversation useful

What would be helpful to tell us?

There is no complicated application process. A little context can help us understand your requirements and consider the next step.

01 / YOUR WORK

Tell us about yourself

Introduce yourself and, where relevant, share your book title, author website, publishing background or current project.

02 / YOUR AMBITION

Explain what you want to achieve

Tell us what you would like to accomplish, whether it concerns your book, readership, publishing plans or wider professional goals.

03 / YOUR CHALLENGE

Describe what is getting in the way

Share the difficulty, unanswered question or opportunity you are trying to understand. You do not need to have a solution already.

What happens next

A considered response begins with your enquiry.

We want the first conversation to establish whether there is a genuine fit between your requirements and what AuthorVeyra can contribute.

01

We consider your message

We review the information you provide to understand your objectives, current situation and the challenge involved.

02

We clarify what is needed

Where further information is necessary, we may ask questions to understand the requirements more accurately.

03

We discuss a possible way forward

If there is a suitable opportunity to work together, we can discuss the proposed scope, responsibilities, expectations and relevant commercial arrangements before any commitment.

Sending an enquiry does not create an obligation to purchase or proceed with any work.
