Our Approach

Understanding the problem is where finding the solution begins.

Every author has a different story, ambition and set of circumstances. When something is not working, an important decision needs to be made or a new opportunity feels difficult to pursue, the first step is understanding what is really happening. That is where our work begins.

Our philosophy

We don't begin with an answer. We begin with your situation.

An author may know exactly what is troubling them. Another may simply know that something is not moving in the direction they hoped.

Sometimes the problem is visible. Sometimes it is connected to other issues that have not yet been recognised. What appears to be one difficulty may have several contributing factors.

We believe it is important to understand the full picture before deciding what should happen next. That means listening carefully, asking relevant questions and examining the circumstances rather than making assumptions.

"Before deciding what an author needs, we first need to understand what the author is facing."

THE AUTHORVEYRA PRINCIPLE

How we work

A thoughtful process for finding the right way forward.

We take each situation on its own merits. Our process helps us understand the challenge, establish what matters and work towards a response that addresses the actual problem.

01

Listen and understand

We begin by listening to the author. We want to understand what they are trying to achieve, what difficulties they are experiencing, what concerns them and what they would like to change.

We also consider the author's background, current circumstances and what has already been attempted.

02

Examine the situation

We look more closely at the circumstances surrounding the challenge. Depending on the situation, this may involve examining relevant materials, existing arrangements, available information, previous decisions and practical limitations.

Our aim is to understand what is happening rather than rely only on the first explanation of the problem.

03

Identify the real problem

Once we understand the situation more clearly, we identify the issues that are affecting progress. We consider which factors matter most, what may be causing the difficulty and whether there are connected problems that also need attention.

This helps us distinguish between symptoms and the underlying issues that need to be addressed.

04

Explore the possibilities

We consider the available options and determine which possibilities are realistic, relevant and worth pursuing.

This may involve research, evaluating alternatives, considering different approaches or understanding what resources and expertise would be required.

We focus on what could genuinely help the author rather than recommending unnecessary activity.

05

Develop a practical solution

With a clearer understanding of the problem and available possibilities, we develop a considered way forward.

The response is shaped around the author's objectives, circumstances, priorities and the practical requirements involved. It may address one particular issue or several connected problems.

06

Put the solution into action

Once the direction and responsibilities have been agreed, we help move the work forward. Depending on what is required, this may involve our direct involvement, collaboration with appropriate professionals or coordination of different activities.

We establish clear expectations about the work, responsibilities, timing and any relevant costs before proceeding.

07

Follow progress and consider what comes next

We maintain communication throughout the agreed work, discuss important developments and help keep attention on the author's objectives.

Where appropriate, we review progress, consider what has changed and identify any further decisions or steps that may be necessary.

Looking at the bigger picture

Sometimes the problem is bigger than it first appears.

An author's immediate concern may be connected to decisions, circumstances or opportunities elsewhere in their publishing journey or wider professional work.

For example, difficulty reaching readers may have connections to how a book is positioned, presented or made available. A project that has stalled may involve unresolved decisions, practical arrangements or people whose involvement is needed.

An author considering a new direction may need to understand the possibilities, the requirements and the risks before deciding how to proceed.

We therefore consider the wider context where it is relevant. We want to understand how the different parts of an author's situation relate to one another.

01

The immediate concern

What is troubling the author or preventing the progress they want?

02

The underlying factors

What circumstances, decisions or obstacles may be contributing to the problem?

03

The wider implications

What else might be affected, and what should be considered before taking action?

04

The way forward

What practical response would best address the situation and support the author's objective?

What authors can expect

Understanding, clear thinking and a considered response.

We want authors to feel that their situation has been properly understood and that the direction being considered has a clear reason behind it.

UNDERSTANDING

Your situation matters

We take your concerns, ambitions and circumstances seriously. We do not assume that we already know what you need.

THOUGHTFULNESS

Decisions with a reason

We consider the available information, examine the relevant issues and explain why a particular direction may be appropriate.

TRANSPARENCY

Clear expectations

We believe in clear communication about what is involved, what can realistically be achieved and what responsibilities each party has.

RELEVANCE

A response shaped around you

We consider your actual circumstances rather than expect you to fit into a predetermined solution.

PRACTICALITY

Progress that makes sense

We focus on realistic next steps, appropriate decisions and actions that relate to the problem being addressed.

COLLABORATION

Working towards the same objective

Where other people or specialist expertise are needed, we aim to establish clear roles and keep the agreed work aligned.
