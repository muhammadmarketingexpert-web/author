Our Approach & Solutions

A thoughtful process. A response shaped around your goals.

Every author's situation is different. We begin by understanding what you want to achieve, examining the challenges involved and identifying the most appropriate way forward. The response may involve strategy, creative development, publishing support, commercial planning or a combination of different services.

How we work

From understanding your challenge to moving the work forward.

We do not assume that every author needs the same service. Our process is designed to understand the actual requirements before recommending or undertaking any work.

01

Understand the ambition

We begin by learning what you want to achieve for your book, readership, catalogue, publishing project or wider author career. We also consider the context behind your situation, including what you have already tried and what progress you want to make.

02

Examine the situation

Where appropriate, we examine the relevant materials, publishing arrangements, commercial presentation, discoverability, existing resources and practical requirements. This helps us distinguish visible problems from underlying issues that may need closer investigation.

03

Identify the opportunity

We identify the challenges and possibilities most relevant to your goals. This may involve improving an existing book, developing a publishing strategy, exploring another market, addressing distribution requirements or considering opportunities connected to your wider work.

We prioritise what matters rather than recommending activity simply because it is available.

04

Develop a suitable response

Based on the findings, we determine what kind of support may be appropriate. Depending on the requirements, this could involve work delivered directly by AuthorVeyra, collaboration with suitable specialists or coordination of different parts of a larger project.

The objective is to build a response around your actual needs, not fit your situation into a predetermined package.

05

Agree on the project and responsibilities

Before work begins, you receive a clear proposal explaining the recommended scope, deliverables, responsibilities, relevant specialist involvement, timing and fees.

Any work, external involvement and payment arrangements are agreed before the project proceeds.

06

Deliver and coordinate the agreed work

Once you approve the proposal, we move into the agreed work. AuthorVeyra carries out the services within its agreed scope and, where necessary, identifies and coordinates appropriate specialist support.

This may involve bringing together different activities, professional services or project requirements so that the agreed parts work towards the same objective.

07

Maintain communication and review progress

Throughout the agreed project, we keep communication focused on progress, important decisions, outstanding requirements and next steps. Where appropriate, we review completed work and discuss further opportunities.

Areas of possibility

Different challenges call for different solutions.

AuthorVeyra considers the wider needs of authors and their work. The areas below illustrate the kinds of challenges we may help address, depending on the project and agreed scope.

01 / BOOK GROWTH

Book positioning & discoverability

Reader alignment, book presentation, metadata, categories, keywords and relevant discovery pathways.

02 / COMMERCIAL PERFORMANCE

Book performance examination

Examining commercial presentation, reader-facing opportunities, purchase friction and connections to an author's wider catalogue.

03 / AUTHOR DEVELOPMENT

Author platform & credibility

Connecting professional expertise, author identity, online presence and relevant authority with the work.

04 / CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT

Creative content

Video, audio, animation and visual storytelling that suit the book, its purpose and its intended audience.

05 / CATALOGUE DEVELOPMENT

Catalogue & series pathways

Helping readers navigate related titles, series, backlist books and other parts of an author's work.

06 / NEW FORMATS

Adaptation & new formats

Exploring how a story, book or body of knowledge might be experienced through another suitable format.

07 / PUBLISHING

Publishing strategy

Examining publishing options, production requirements, routes to market and the decisions involved in moving a publishing project forward.

08 / MARKET EXPANSION

International market entry

Exploring opportunities to introduce books and author work into additional markets, including the requirements, potential routes and relevant local considerations.

09 / DISTRIBUTION

Distribution & fulfilment

Examining availability, ordering arrangements, shipping, fulfilment and suitable distribution options for the intended market.

10 / PARTNERSHIPS

Publishing partnerships

Exploring relevant relationships with publishers, distributors, retailers, organisations and other professional partners.

11 / BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Wider commercial opportunities

Considering how books, knowledge, professional experience and intellectual property may support additional commercial opportunities.

12 / PROJECT COORDINATION

Project & specialist coordination

Helping organise the different services, people, responsibilities and activities involved in a connected publishing or author project.

Book Revenue Engine™

Book Revenue Engine™ is one focused commercial-growth method within AuthorVeyra. It examines how a book is positioned and presented to readers, including discoverability, listing quality, reader confidence and relevant connections to an author's wider catalogue.

It is one part of our wider author-solutions approach, not the whole of what AuthorVeyra does. It does not promise specific sales, rankings or revenue outcomes.

How projects move forward

The right people, responsibilities and work for each project.

Not every requirement needs the same delivery arrangement. Our role depends on the nature of the work, the capabilities required and what has been agreed with the author.

DIRECT SUPPORT

Work delivered by AuthorVeyra

Where a requirement falls within our capabilities and agreed scope, we undertake the work directly and remain responsible for the agreed deliverables.

SPECIALIST SUPPORT

Suitable professional expertise

When a project requires expertise or services outside our direct delivery capabilities, we may identify suitable specialists for consideration and agree how their involvement will be handled.

PROJECT COORDINATION

Connected work and responsibilities

Where agreed, we help coordinate different parts of a project, clarify responsibilities and keep activities aligned with the author's objectives.

The scope of AuthorVeyra's responsibility, the role of any external provider and the relevant commercial arrangements are clarified before work begins.
