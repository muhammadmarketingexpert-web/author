A wider perspective

Sometimes the next step is bigger than a marketing plan.

An author may be preparing to enter a new country, reviewing publishing options, looking for suitable distribution arrangements or trying to bring several parts of a project together.

These challenges are connected. A market opportunity may depend on distribution. A launch may require a suitable website, fulfilment arrangements and a clear commercial plan. The right response starts with understanding the author's objective and the practical requirements behind it.

“A clear publishing direction brings the right decisions, people and practical steps into focus.”

How our role is shaped

The right support, clearly defined.

Not every project requires the same level or type of involvement. Our role is established around the actual needs of the author and the requirements of the work.

Depending on the agreed scope, AuthorVeyra may provide strategic guidance, deliver services directly, research and identify potential providers, or coordinate specialists and project activities.

Where external providers are involved, their responsibilities, fees, terms and relationship to the project should be made clear before any work is commissioned.
