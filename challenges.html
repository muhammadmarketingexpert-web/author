Author Challenges

Every challenge has a context. Every author has a different next step.

Some authors know exactly what is holding them back. Others only know that the results, reach or opportunities do not yet match the work they have created. Their challenges may involve a book, a publishing decision, a commercial opportunity or the wider business behind their work. Both are good places to begin.

Books, readers and author identity

Challenges surrounding the book and its audience

A book can have valuable ideas and a clearly defined purpose, yet still face obstacles in how it is positioned, presented, discovered and experienced by readers.

01 / DISCOVERY

Readers are not finding the book

Could the book's positioning, metadata, categories, presentation or discovery pathways be making it harder for the right readers to encounter it?

02 / CONNECTION

Attention is not becoming interest

Does the book page communicate its value clearly? Are readers given a compelling reason to explore the book and consider purchasing it?

03 / IDENTITY

The author's expertise is underrepresented

Could the author's experience, credentials, ideas or professional work be connected more effectively with the book and its intended audience?

04 / CATALOGUE

Books are working in isolation

Are related titles, series, backlist books or complementary subjects connected in a way that encourages readers to explore more of the author's work?

05 / FORMAT

The work could reach people differently

Would audio, video, animation, visual storytelling or adaptation open a relevant new way for people to experience the author's work?

06 / DIRECTION

The next move is unclear

Are there several possible paths, but no clear way to decide which opportunity deserves attention first?

Publishing, business and operations

Challenges beyond the individual book

An author's ambitions may involve more than marketing or reader discovery. Publishing decisions, commercial arrangements, distribution and operational requirements can all influence how far a book or author business can go.

07 / PUBLISHING STRATEGY

Important publishing decisions need clarity

Is the author uncertain about publishing options, the right route to market, production requirements or the best way to move a publishing project forward?

08 / INTERNATIONAL MARKETS

The book needs to reach a new market

Does the author want to introduce their work to readers in another country, but need a clearer understanding of market requirements, local opportunities and possible entry routes?

09 / DISTRIBUTION

Getting the book to readers is complicated

Are availability, ordering arrangements, shipping, fulfilment or distribution options creating obstacles between the book and its intended market?

10 / PARTNERSHIPS

The right publishing connections are missing

Could suitable publishers, distributors, retailers, organisations or other professional relationships help create opportunities for the author's work?

11 / BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

The author's wider opportunities are not fully developed

Could the author's books, knowledge, professional experience or intellectual property support additional commercial opportunities or new forms of engagement?

12 / PROJECT COORDINATION

Too many moving parts are difficult to manage

Are several people, services or decisions involved in the same project, without a clear plan for how the different parts should work together?

A wider perspective

Sometimes the problem is not where you first expect it to be.

A book that is struggling to reach readers may need more than promotional activity. Its positioning, distribution, availability or market strategy may also deserve attention.

Similarly, an author looking to enter a new market may need several connected elements to work together, from publishing decisions and commercial planning to distribution and professional coordination.

That is why we look beyond the immediate difficulty to understand the wider situation before considering an appropriate response.
