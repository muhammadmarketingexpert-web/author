Author Challenges

Every challenge has a context. Every author has a different next step.

Some authors know exactly what is holding them back. Others only know that the results, reach or opportunities do not yet match the work they have created. Both are good places to begin.

Challenges we explore

What may be getting in the way?

These are not fixed service categories or assumptions about your situation. They are examples of questions we can investigate together.

01 / DISCOVERY

Readers are not finding the book

Could the book's positioning, metadata, categories, presentation or discovery pathways be making it harder for the right readers to encounter it?

02 / CONNECTION

Attention is not becoming interest

Does the book page communicate its value clearly? Are readers given a compelling reason to explore further?

03 / IDENTITY

The author's expertise is underrepresented

Could the author's experience, credentials, ideas or professional work be connected more effectively with the book?

04 / CATALOGUE

Books are working in isolation

Are related titles, series, backlist books or complementary subjects connected in a way readers can follow?

05 / FORMAT

The work could reach people differently

Would audio, video, animation, visual storytelling or adaptation open a relevant new way to experience the work?

06 / DIRECTION

The next move is unclear

Are there several possible paths, but no clear way to decide which deserves attention first?

A useful first conversation

You don't need to diagnose the problem before contacting us.

Tell us what you want for your book or author career, and describe what feels difficult at the moment. We can use that context to understand where a closer examination may help.
