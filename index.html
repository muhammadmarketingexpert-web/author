Not every author needs the same answer.

A book may need to reach a clearer readership. An author may need a stronger way to present their expertise. A body of work may hold opportunities that are not yet connected.

Sometimes the challenge extends beyond the book itself. An author may need to explore a new publishing market, understand distribution options, develop a stronger platform or coordinate several parts of a publishing project.
