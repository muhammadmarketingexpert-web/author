Why AUTHORVEYRA GLOBAL exists

Authors deserve more than a list of services.

An author's problem does not always fit neatly into a predefined service or package. Sometimes the real issue is not immediately clear. Sometimes several different challenges are connected.

AUTHORVEYRA GLOBAL was established as a company to bring a team-based, considered approach to the challenges authors encounter throughout their work.

Our professional standards

A professional relationship should be clear from the beginning.

Authors should be able to understand who they are working with, what has been agreed and how their project will be handled. These principles should guide every AuthorVeyra engagement.

01 / CLARITY

Clear expectations

The proposed work, expected deliverables, responsibilities, timing and costs should be explained before an author makes a commitment.

02 / HONESTY

Considered recommendations

Recommendations should reflect the author's actual situation. We should not promise guaranteed sales, results or outcomes.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Defined responsibilities

Authors should know who is responsible for agreed work, how any external specialists are involved and how project communication will be handled.

04 / RESPECT

Respect for authors and their work

An author's creative work, materials, decisions and professional interests should be treated with respect.

05 / TRANSPARENCY

Open communication

Important project decisions, changes and requirements should be communicated clearly rather than left uncertain.

06 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Agreed work matters

Responsibilities and progress should be handled in line with the commitments made for each agreed project.
