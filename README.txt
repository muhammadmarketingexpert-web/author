PUBLISHING
1. Upload every file in this folder to your hosting account's public web root (often public_html).
2. Keep all files together in the same folder.
3. Point authorveyr.com to that hosting account.
4. The contact form uses FormSubmit and is configured for info@authorveyr.com.
5. On the first form submission, FormSubmit may send a verification email to info@authorveyr.com. Open that email and confirm the address before relying on form delivery.
6. If your live site uses www or another canonical URL, update the _next value in contact.html accordingly.
7. Test the contact form after activation.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE
- Refund Policy and Terms & Conditions are draft documents, not legal advice. Complete the governing-law placeholder in terms.html and have both pages reviewed for the company's registration jurisdiction and actual business practices.
- Add a privacy notice if required by your applicable law and actual data-handling practices.
- Confirm that info@authorveyr.com is an active inbox.
- The website uses Google Fonts and will fall back to system fonts if external font loading is unavailable.
